What it reads

Constructor and driver championship position, last three and five finishes, form at this circuit, reliability, and the grid when qualifying has run.

No peeking

Features are rebuilt from the championship as it stood after the previous round, so a prediction for an upcoming race uses nothing from the future.

Honest limits

Safety cars, weather and first-lap contact are not predictable. Read the order as a ranking, not a promise — the score is an expected finishing position.
